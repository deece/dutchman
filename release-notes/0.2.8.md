Dutchman Convert 0.2.8 keeps more of a design's history and is more honest about what it kept: extrudes no longer sweep the wrong sketch region, more Fusion and Inventor features stay editable, and a feature SolidCraft refuses is reported as SolidCraft's, with a link. Result cards fit the window and can be reported from directly, the Update button always checks for the newest release, and the command line gains a few fixes asked for in use.

## What's new

- **Right profiles, checked.** Fusion extrudes are read with the profile their end face proves, instead of a guess that could pick the wrong region. Dutchman also compares a kept extrude's swept region with the source's end face where it can, and marks the extrude unverified where it can't.
- **More history kept.** Extrudes that start or end at a face of an earlier state, end at a face's plane, or start at a plane keep their history. So do holes placed at several sketch points, counterbores and countersinks with a drill point (SolidCraft 0.22.1 and newer), fillets with several radius groups, and bodies a file leaves unnamed, as Inventor parts do.
- **Clear reasons.** A feature SolidCraft won't rebuild is reported as "SolidCraft refused …", with a category and the upstream issue where one exists, such as a fillet wider than the face beside it. Messages name the selection form they can't handle, and anonymous reports list blocked features by timeline position and form.
- **Cards that fit, and report from them.** Result cards fit the window: a failed file's help folds under **Help us fix it**, and **Ask your AI about this file** shows a short prompt in a small box. Any result with a loss has **Report this file…**.
- **Finding SolidCraft.** The desktop app and the command line look for SolidCraft the same way, including per-user installs. When it isn't found, the app and reports say where they looked. `SOLIDCRAFT_CLI` is documented.
- **Command line.** `convert` writes `--report-anon` too, `convert-all` warns when an argument looks like an output folder, progress shows only in a terminal, and `--write-replay` writes the replay script next to the design.
- **Updates.** **Update** asks for the newest release each time it's pressed and shows its notes properly formatted.

## Known issues

- A feature can still replay from the wrong sketch region and count as kept when Dutchman can't check it against the source; such extrudes are marked unverified in the JSON report. A check for every case waits on the decoder (Dutchman, #489; cadmpeg #944).
- A large Fusion multi-document archive can still need close to 24 GB while reading (Dutchman, #227).
- Some parts of a large Fusion assembly can be placed away from the published assembly (Dutchman, #228).
- A Fusion design with Remove features can keep the removed bodies (Dutchman, #485).
- An extrude that ends at a work plane or point after earlier features is still kept as geometry (Dutchman, #486).
- SolidWorks mates are not converted, and most of a large SolidWorks assembly converts as geometry (cadmpeg, #487).
- The estimate before a conversion can be well under the real peak on large assemblies (Dutchman, #388).
